Questionable Sphere
About

A world of questions, floating.

Questionable Sphere is a place where questions float freely and people can discover conversations they might never have found otherwise.

The idea

Most conversation online is arranged as a feed: newest first, loudest first. Questionable Sphere is arranged as a small world. Questions appear as points on a sphere. You turn it, zoom, and arrive somewhere you did not plan to go.

The product is meant to feel quiet. There is no traditional account required to look around. There is no score, no follower graph, and no advertising layer in this prototype.

How it feels

You can leave a question, sit with someone else’s, send a short reply, or ask to be taken somewhere at random. Search exists for when a word is already in mind. Themes exist so the room can be light or dark.

What this version is

This is a live, independent project. Questions you leave are stored on a real server for 17 hours, then automatically deleted. There are no accounts, no emails required, and no analytics. The only thing stored on your device is your theme preference and your chosen sphere skin.

Questionable Sphere is not trying to replace every kind of conversation. It is trying to make a few questions easier to find, and a few thoughts easier to leave behind.

FAQ

Questions about the sphere

Short answers that match what the product does today.

What is Questionable Sphere?

A visual place for questions. Each point on the sphere is a thought you can open, sit with, or answer.

Do I need an account?

No. You can explore, leave a question, reply, search, and contact the project without creating an account.

Do I need an email address?

Not for ordinary use of this prototype. A future API may require an email if you request developer access.

How long does a question stay visible?

In this prototype, questions stay in the local sphere until you clear your browser data. A later version may use a public visibility window (for example, a few days). Leaving public view would not automatically mean every copy is erased from every system.

Can I submit anonymously?

Yes. This version does not ask for your name, email, or password to leave a question.

How does the sphere work?

It is a 3D object you can rotate a full 360° and zoom. Points are placed on the surface. Click a point to open it. The camera can travel to that point.

Can I search questions?

Yes. Open settings and choose “Search the sphere.” Matching points can be opened directly.

How does random discovery work?

“Take me somewhere” chooses a question you have not just seen, flies the camera there, and opens it.

When you leave a question or reply to one, the text is stored on our server so other people can see it while it is alive. After roughly 17 hours, it is deleted automatically. We also store the time it was posted, so we can show the sphere in order and know when to delete it.

We do not collect your name. We do not collect your email. We do not collect your phone number. We do not ask you to create an account. We do not know who you are.

Your theme preference (light or dark) and your chosen sphere skin are saved in your own browser. That is all. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser data. We never see them.
